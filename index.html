Projects

Multimodal Model Zoo
A structured and searchable database of 114+ Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), organized by generation paradigm (Auto-Regressive, Diffusion, and hybrid AR & Diffusion). Covers diverse modalities, including image, text, video, speech, and 3D, and spans research areas including vision-language, audio-language, autonomous driving, and 3D scene understanding. Each model entry provides detailed information on architecture, tasks, benchmarks, and links to papers and code.
